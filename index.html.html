About CoraVergel Resort

CoraVergel Resort is a leisure destination in Barangay Barosong, Tigbauan, Iloilo, Philippines. It offers a place for visitors to relax, spend quality time with loved ones, and enjoy a break from their daily routines.

Whether you are planning a family outing, a gathering with friends, or a relaxing day away from the city, CoraVergel Resort is a destination worth exploring.
